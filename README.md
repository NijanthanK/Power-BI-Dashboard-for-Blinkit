# Power-BI-Dashboard-for-Blinkit
Multi-Metric Sales Insights 

I recently built a retail performance dashboard in Power BI using Blinkit’s data to visualize key business metrics and draw actionable insights. The goal was to understand how outlet types, product categories, and fat content impact sales.

 What I Analyzed:
 Total Sales by Fat Content & Outlet Type
 Item Type Revenue Distribution
 Year-wise Performance by Establishment Date
 Tier-wise Outlet Location Insights
 Size-wise Sales Contribution

 Multi-Metric Outlet Type KPIs including:
 Total Sales
 Number of Items
 Average Sales
 Customer Rating
 Item Visibility Score

 Insights Discovered:
 Low Fat products lead in both quantity and revenue
 Tier 3 outlets drive the most revenue
 Medium-sized outlets strike a balance between reach and performance
 Supermarket Type1 has highest item count but not the highest avg sales
 Ratings remain consistent across outlet types (Avg: 4.0)
 Tools Used: Power BI | Excel | DAX
 Focus: Retail Sales & Business Intelligence
